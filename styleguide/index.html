    Motifs and rules

    • The arch. The window on the book cover frames the portrait and the “Start here” doors.
    • Navy duotone. Archive photos and video stills are toned to one palette, so mixed-quality images read as a series.
    • Pattern. A fine eight-point geometric line at 6% opacity, on navy bands only.
    • Motion. Fades and 8px rises once per element; switched off under reduced-motion settings. No carousels, parallax or popups.
    • Special characters. Titles stay in sentence case so ā, ḥ, ʿ and ʾ keep their shape; ﷺ always uses Amiri and has an accessible name.
