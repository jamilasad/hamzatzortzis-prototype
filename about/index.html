    Biography

    The story so far

    Born in London to a Greek family, Hamza embraced Islam in 2002. Since then his work has centred on the questions that matter most: whether God exists, why the Qur’an is His word, and what it means to know, love and worship Him.

    He studied philosophy at the University of London, earning a postgraduate certificate, an MA and an MRes, and completed doctoral research at Cardiff University on the Qur’an and its verses about the natural world. His academic writing, published as Andreas Tzortzis, includes a peer-reviewed article in Zygon: Journal of Religion and Science (2025).

    Through the 2010s he debated leading academics and public intellectuals on Islam and atheism, among them Professor Lawrence Krauss, Professor Peter Simons, Professor Simon Blackburn and Dan Barker. His 2016 book, The Divine Reality, drew that work together into a sustained case for God, the Qur’an and the Prophethood of Muhammad ﷺ.

    From 2017 to 2020 he was CEO of iERA. Today he leads Sapience Institute, where he teaches on the Intellectual Ambassador Programme, founded Lighthouse Mentoring and lectures at universities and institutions worldwide.

    He loves reading, writing poetry, travelling and martial arts: he is a trained boxer and Wing Chun practitioner. Two sayings of the Prophet ﷺ he often returns to are “compassion beautifies everything” and “love for humanity what you love for yourself”.
