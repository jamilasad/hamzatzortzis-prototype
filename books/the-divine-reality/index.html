    The Divine Reality

    God, Islam & the Mirage of Atheism

    ★★★★★4.45 on Goodreads from 992 ratings (Nov 2026)

    In The Divine Reality, Hamza Andreas Tzortzis provides a compelling case for the rational and spiritual foundations of Islam, while intelligently and compassionately deconstructing atheism.

    Join him on an existential, spiritual and rational journey that articulates powerful arguments for the existence of God, the Qur’an, the Prophethood of Muhammad ﷺ, and why we must know, love and worship God. He addresses academic and popular objections while showing how contemporary atheism is based on false assumptions about reality.

    Readers’ questions

    Questions about the book

    In the chapter on consciousness you briefly mention Brian Loar’s phenomenal concept strategy. Why do you think his view fails?

    An examination of Loar’s phenomenal concept strategy is in the essay on Frank Jackson’s knowledge argument, which explains why Loar’s strategy is not decisive. Read the essay.

    Is the book written for Muslims or for atheists?

    Both. It is written so that a sceptical reader can follow every argument, while giving Muslims a structured account of the rational and spiritual foundations of their faith.

    How does the 2019 edition differ from the 2016 first edition?

    It adds responses to newer objections, reworks many chapters and corrects the print errata listed opposite.
